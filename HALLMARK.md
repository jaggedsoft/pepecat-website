## Signature decisions

- Floating pill navigation (N5) sits over a dark canvas with two static phosphor blooms and a fixed grain overlay.
- The hero is a split photographic stage: sentence-case display type, one archive CTA, and a double-bezel mascot frame.
- The archive is an ecosystem index: a featured rail of new drops, then a shuffle mosaic from the local vault.
- Coral UI accent was replaced with a single phosphor green so the chrome matches the artwork without turning neon.
- Token facts stay a compact receipt. The footer is a statement line, not a sitemap.
- Motion is limited to opacity fades (hero settle, shuffle reorder) and is removed under reduced-motion.
